Sustaining Our Future

Empowering Players, Protecting Families

This section details why our fundraising model shifts focus toward corporate partnerships. As pitch fees, equipment costs, and league registration rise annually, relying solely on family donations is unsustainable during a cost-of-living crisis. Our aim is to build strong relationships with local businesses to cover essential equipment and enrich player growth without burdening households.

🛡️

Weather-Proofing the Team

Playing winter football in Scotland means facing sub-zero temperatures, sleet, and rain. Securing £3,000 ensures every girl gets high-spec winter sideline jackets, keeping substitutes warm, healthy, and focused.

💬

Building Life Skills & Confidence

Football is our vehicle for personal growth. By actively engaging players in outreach, presentation, and digital media creation, we build leadership, teamwork, communication, and resilience off the pitch.

Mission Statement

"We want to keep the team financially healthy so the girls always have the right kit and get to enjoy experiences that help them develop into confident young leaders."

Four Core Streams

The Game Plan

This section details our four structured initiatives. Each plan is designed to provide clear, tangible commercial value to local businesses while ensuring parents face no compulsory fundraising burdens.

Initiative 01 Target: £1,000

1. The Business Football Card

We replace traditional £2 family scratch cards (where parents end up buying remaining squares) with a B2B model. Players approach local businesses to buy a square for £50.

The Business Reward

The winning business receives official kit sponsorship—placing their brand logo prominently on the girls' essential winter gear, alongside social media features and photo ops.

Revenue Comparison: Family vs Business Card
Initiative 02 Target: £900 – £2,000

2. The Partnership Programme

A structured multi-tier sponsorship model offering local businesses direct advertising exposure to over 200 local families and friends through simple, predictable flat-rate packages.

💡 Tax-Deductible Advertising

Sponsorship payments for advertising are legitimate business expenses and can be 100% tax-deductible against company profits.

Initiative 03 Up to £150 / Switch

3. The UW Connector

Generate funds while saving money at home. By completing a quick household utility review and bundling services through Utility Warehouse, families reduce monthly bills—and up to £150 per referral goes directly into the team account.

Family Utility Benefit: Reduces household monthly expenditure
Club Referral Payout: Up to £150 per switch
10 Switch Potential: £1,500 injected into team fund
Initiative 04 Target: £2,500+ Single Day

4. Team Car Boot Sale

Hosting a community car boot event where public cars (£20), small businesses (£50), and food vendors (£100) rent pitch space. Combined with admission fees and raffles, it delivers single-day impact.

📍 Motherwell Venue Search: We are actively seeking a suitable large car park or school space around Motherwell to host safely.
